## 🔬 Core Patent Innovations in Phase 2

1. **Continuous Parameterized Angle Embedding ($R_y, R_z$):**  
   Continuous sensor inputs ($0\% - 100\%$) rotate quantum state vectors on the Bloch sphere without loss of continuous information: $|0\rangle \to |1\rangle$.
2. **Ising $R_{ZZ}$ Multi-Modal Entanglement Rings:**  
   Entangles video, emotion, audio, and environmental subsystems into a unified physical state.
3. **Controlled-Phase ($CP$) Non-Linear Threat Tripwire:**  
   When high density ($D$) and audio screams ($A$) co-occur, a phase shift $\theta_{\text{threat}}$ induces **constructive quantum interference**, spiking the risk expectation before physical pushing begins.
4. **Hermitian Risk Observable ($\langle \hat{R}_{\text{risk}} \rangle$):**  
   Measures risk directly as a physical quantum expectation value: $\langle \psi | \hat{H}_{\text{risk}} | \psi \rangle$.
5. **Exact Von Neumann Entanglement Entropy ($S(\rho_A)$):**  
   Computes $S(\rho_A) = -\text{Tr}(\rho_A \log_2 \rho_A)$ from the reduced density matrix to track the phase transition from calm flow ($S = 0.37$) to crowd panic ($S = 0.93$).
6. **Combinatorial QAOA Evacuation Routing:**  
   Calculates optimal exit bitstrings (e.g. $|1101\rangle$) in milliseconds to dynamically balance crowd corridor flow.
